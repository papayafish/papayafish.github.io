OUR STORY

台灣的海裡，In Taiwan's seas,
真的有一隻叫「木瓜」的魚there really is a fish called “Papaya”

牠是粒突箱魨。方方的身體、檸檬黃的顏色、滿身小黑點，在珊瑚礁之間不急不徐地游。我們叫牠「小木瓜」。It's the yellow boxfish: a little square body, lemon yellow, covered in tiny black dots, drifting unhurried between the corals. We call it “Little Papaya.”

我們借了牠的名字，做給旅行的人、給愛海的人，可以一直帶在身邊的東西。We borrowed its name to make things for travelers and ocean lovers — things to carry with you, always.

Ostracion cubicus

最少可以做多少？What's the minimum order?+

依品項不同，很多品項少量也能做。告訴我們大概的數量和預算，我們幫你找最適合的做法。It depends on the item — many can be made in small runs. Tell us your rough quantity and budget, and we'll find the best way.

從詢問到交貨要多久？How long from inquiry to delivery?+

依品項和數量而定。打樣確認後才會進大貨生產；詢問時告訴我們需要的日期，我們會先確認時間來不來得及。It depends on the item and quantity. Full production starts once the sample is approved — tell us your date up front and we'll confirm the timing first.

可以只做設計，或只做生產嗎？Can you do just design, or just production?+

都可以。只要設計，我們交設計檔；已經有設計，我們也可以直接幫你生產。Either works. Design only: we deliver the files. Already have a design: we can produce it for you.

可以開發票嗎？Do you issue invoices?+

公司正在設立中，設立完成後即可開立統一發票。We’re currently registering the company — official invoices will be available once that’s complete.
